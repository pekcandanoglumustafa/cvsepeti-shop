The price range for traffic cones is wide — two cones of the same height can differ threefold in price. Knowing where this difference comes from prevents you from buying a product that is more expensive than necessary or too cheap to do the job.

Factors that determine the price

Height and amount of plastic. A 90 cm cone contains about three times the raw material of a 50 cm cone. This is the main reason for the price difference.

Body weight. There are 1.5 kg and 3 kg cones of the same height. The heavier one has a thicker wall, is more impact-resistant and is blown around less by the wind.

Weight base. A weight ring made from recycled rubber costs 150-300 TL on its own. Weighted models carry this difference.

Reflective band. Glass bead tape is cheap; microprismatic tape is expensive. RA2 class tape costs two to three times as much as RA1.

Material. PVC is flexible and does not break; PPC is rigid and cheaper. PVC models are generally 20-30% more expensive.

Is a weighted cone worth the extra cost?

It depends. There is one question to decide: does the cone move out of place?

A weighted model is needed when:
- Open areas exposed to wind, bridges, open motorways
- Heavy vehicle traffic — the air current from passing lorries blows cones away
- Applications where the cone will stay in place for days

A non-weighted model is sufficient when:
- Indoor car parks and interiors
- Windless, sheltered areas
- Short-term, supervised applications

The weighted cone also has a disadvantage: it is hard to carry. A 90 cm weighted cone weighs 8-10 kg, and carrying 20 of them is serious work.

Which cone do public technical specifications require?

The specification commonly found in municipal and public purchases is:

  • Height: 75 cm
  • Weight: 4 kg
  • Standard: KGM 2013 Highways Technical Specification Section 503, Type-1
  • Reflective: double band, RA2 class
  • There may be a request for the institution's logo or printing

A cone meeting this specification costs 700-900 TL on the market. Economy models do not meet this specification — if offered, they are rejected at the inspection stage.

Bulk purchase discount rates

Common tiers in the sector:

  • 1–24 units: list price
  • 25–99 units: 10–15% discount
  • 100–499 units: 20–30% discount
  • Over 500 units: project pricing

The bulk purchase advantage is greater for cones than for delineators because cones nest inside each other, so shipping costs do not rise proportionally.

20 units of the 75 cm cone come to about three times the desi of a single unit — not 20 times. This significantly reduces the unit shipping cost for bulk purchases.

What to watch for in cheap cones

Wall thickness. A thin body is crushed the first time a vehicle runs over it and does not regain its shape. Squeeze the cone by hand; if it crushes easily, it is thin.

Band attachment. A band that is only stuck to the surface soon peels off. In a quality product, the band sits in a channel in the body.

Colour quality. Plastic without a UV additive fades to a pale orange by the end of one summer, reducing visibility.

Base flatness. A warped base makes the cone unstable, and it tips over in a light wind.

Should you rent or buy cones?

For use a few times a year for a few days, renting may make sense. However, the traffic equipment rental market in Türkiye is limited.

For continuous or frequent use, buying is always more economical. A cone lasts 3-5 years with proper use; over this period, rental fees exceed the purchase price many times over.
