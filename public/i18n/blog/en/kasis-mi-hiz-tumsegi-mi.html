Both slow vehicles down, but they are different jobs. The wrong choice leads either to unnecessary cost or to an installation that does not work.

What is the difference between a speed bump and a speed hump?

A speed bump is a factory-made modular product. It is made of rubber or plastic, comes in segments and is screwed to the ground with wall plugs. It can be removed and relocated.

A speed hump is built on site. Asphalt or concrete is poured to create a permanent raised section on the road. Removing it requires breaking it up.

When is a speed bump used?

  • Residential complexes and apartment car parks
  • Workplace and factory sites
  • Shopping mall and hospital car parks
  • Private property
  • Temporary applications

The speed bump's biggest advantages are fast installation and reversibility. If it has been put in the wrong place, it can be removed and relocated. It does not cause permanent damage to the surface.

Because it is narrow, the vehicle feels a sharp warning when it hits the speed bump. This is the desired effect — the driver approaches more slowly the next time.

When is a speed hump used?

  • Public roads
  • Main routes in front of schools and hospitals
  • Bus routes
  • High-traffic roads

Because the hump is wide and gradual, vehicles pass over it more smoothly. This matters for long vehicles such as buses and ambulances — a narrow speed bump causes a sharp jolt for these vehicles.

On public roads, building speed reducers requires permission from the municipality and the highways authority and must comply with standards.

How is the cost of a speed bump calculated?

Speed bumps are sold by the segment. A standard segment is 50 cm wide.

If the road is 6 metres wide: 12 segments + 2 end caps

End caps are sloped and prevent bicycles and motorcycles passing at the edge from catching on it. They should not be skipped.

Installation requires 4 screws per segment, i.e. 48 screws and wall plugs for a 6-metre installation. In most products these are included in the package.

Is a warning sign needed before a speed bump?

It is required on public roads. On private property it is not mandatory but strongly recommended.

The warning sign is placed 30-50 metres before the speed bump. In an indoor car park, 10-15 metres is enough.

If there is night-time use, both the sign and the speed bump should be reflective. Speed bump models with cat's eyes carry prismatic reflectors and are visible from a distance in the dark.

Does a speed bump damage vehicles?

Not when it is the right height and is crossed at the right speed.

There is no problem for a passenger car crossing a 3 cm speed bump at 20 km/h. If the same speed bump is crossed at 50 km/h, the suspension is strained — but that is precisely the purpose of the speed bump: it forces the driver to slow down.

With speed bumps of 5 cm and above, there may be bumper contact on low vehicles. Where there is mixed vehicle traffic, the 5 cm limit should not be exceeded.

Sloped edges and the use of end caps prevent wheels from catching on the side.
