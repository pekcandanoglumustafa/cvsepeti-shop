A speed bump is the most effective way to slow vehicles physically. Unlike signs and warning markings, it does not leave it up to the driver — to get over it, the driver has to slow down.

How high should a speed bump be?

Height directly determines the target speed. A higher speed bump slows vehicles more sharply, but the risk of damage to the underside of the vehicle also increases.

HeightTarget speedArea of use
3 cm20 km/hResidential complexes, car parks, hospitals
4 cm15 km/hAround schools, pedestrian crossings
5 cm10 km/hFactory sites, warehouse entrances, customs

Speed bumps above 5 cm cause bumper contact on low vehicles. For this reason, the 5 cm limit should not be exceeded where there is mixed vehicle traffic.

Rubber or plastic speed bump?

Rubber speed bumps are made from recycled rubber. They are heavy, stay firmly in place, withstand heavy vehicle traffic and make no noise. They are the right choice for continuous use.

Plastic speed bumps are light, easy to install and low-cost. They are sufficient for light traffic and temporary applications. They can crack under heavy vehicles.

At a factory entrance with heavy truck traffic, a plastic speed bump deforms within a few months. In the same location, a rubber speed bump lasts for years.

How is a speed bump installed?

Speed bumps consist of modular segments. Each segment is about 50 cm wide, and segments are joined side by side to reach the road width. Sloped end caps are used at both ends — these prevent vehicles and bicycles passing at the edge from catching on it.

For installation, wall plugs are driven into the asphalt or concrete. Usually 4 screws are used per segment. If the ground is uneven, the speed bump will not sit properly and will loosen over time, so checking the surface before installation is important.

A road 6 metres wide needs 12 segments and 2 end caps.

Is a warning sign mandatory before a speed bump?

According to the Highway Traffic Regulation (Karayolları Trafik Yönetmeliği), there must be a warning sign before a speed bump on public roads. There is no such requirement on private property, but it is recommended for occupational safety.

The warning sign is placed 30-50 metres before the speed bump. For night-time visibility, the sign must be reflective.

What is a speed bump with cat's eyes?

Speed bumps with cat's eyes are models fitted with prismatic reflectors. By reflecting light from headlights back, they make the speed bump visible from a distance at night.

On unlit roads and in areas with night-time traffic, a model with cat's eyes should be chosen. In a lit car park, the standard model is sufficient.
