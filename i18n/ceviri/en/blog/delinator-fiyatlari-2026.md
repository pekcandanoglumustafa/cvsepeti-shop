The most common mistake when buying delineators is looking only at the price. If there is a 30% price difference between two delineators, there is a reason — and that reason usually shows up in the third year.

This guide explains what determines the price, when a cheap model is sufficient and when the more expensive one is actually more economical.

## How much do delineators cost?

As of 2026, market ranges are as follows (excluding VAT, per unit):

| Height | Economy (PE) | Standard (TPU/TPE) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 cm | 280–340 TL | 340–420 TL |
| 45–50 cm | 350–430 TL | 430–520 TL |
| 70–75 cm | 420–520 TL | 520–650 TL |
| 100 cm | 600–720 TL | 720–900 TL |

These ranges include installation screws and wall plugs. If screws are sold separately, add 15-25 TL per unit.

For models with solar flashers, sign brackets or chain connections, the price increases by 150-400 TL per accessory.

## Five factors that determine the price

**Material.** TPU is the most expensive, TPE is mid-range and PE is the cheapest. The difference comes from raw material cost and is directly reflected in service life.

**Height.** Each 25 cm increase adds about 20% to the price. It is not just the amount of plastic — the base weight also has to increase.

**Number and class of reflective bands.** Microprismatic tape is 2-3 times more expensive than glass bead tape, but night-time visibility is markedly higher.

**Base type.** A fixed flange base is cheap; a weighted-base model is expensive. The weight base is usually made of recycled rubber and costs 150-250 TL on its own.

**Quantity.** For purchases above 50 units, project pricing 15-25% below list price applies. For bulk purchases above 200 units, the discount increases further.

## TPU or PE — which is more economical?

The answer to this question lies not in the purchase price but in the three-year total cost.

Let's take an example. You are buying 100 units of the 75 cm delineator:

- **PE option:** 460 TL each, 46.000 TL in total. Average life 1.5-2 years. One repeat purchase needed within three years → 92.000 TL
- **TPU option:** 580 TL each, 58.000 TL in total. Average life 4-5 years. No additional purchase within three years → 58.000 TL

In locations with vehicle contact, TPU works out 37% cheaper over three years. In an indoor car park or a low-impact area, however, PE can be chosen — the difference in service life is not as large there.

In inland regions of Türkiye, winter temperatures can drop to minus 15 degrees. PE becomes brittle at this temperature and breaks instead of bending on impact. In provinces such as Konya, Ankara and Erzurum, TPU or TPE should be chosen.

## How much discount is there for wholesale delineator purchases?

Common tiers in the sector are:

- 1-24 units: list price
- 25-49 units: 5-10% discount
- 50-199 units: 15-25% discount
- Over 200 units: project pricing, shipping usually included

In public tenders, quotes are calculated separately — technical specification requirements affect the cost.

Factor in shipping costs for bulk purchases as well. Delineators do not nest like cones; they are laid side by side. 100 units of the 75 cm delineator come to about 35 desi.

## What to watch for in cheap delineators

There are very cheap models on the market, and not all of them are bad. But check the following:

**How the reflective band is attached.** A band that is only stuck on peels off within a few months. In a quality product, the band sits in a channel recessed into the body.

**Base thickness.** A thin base cracks when the installation screw is tightened. It should be at least 8 mm.

**Body wall thickness.** A thin body is permanently deformed at the first impact and does not return to its original shape.

**UV additive.** Plastic without a UV stabiliser loses its colour and becomes brittle by the end of one summer. Look for "UV stabilised" in the product description.

## Is a delineator or a cone cheaper?

Comparing a delineator and a cone of the same height, the cone is usually cheaper. However, the two are designed for different jobs.

Cones are for temporary applications — they are moved, collected and stored. Delineators are for permanent applications — they are fixed to the ground and stay in place for months.

If you try to separate lanes with cones, they shift in the wind and in vehicle air currents and constantly need repositioning. This labour cost soon exceeds the price difference.

If you need permanent separation, use delineators; if you need temporary guidance, use cones.
