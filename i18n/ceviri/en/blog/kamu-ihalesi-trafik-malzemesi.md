Selling traffic equipment to public institutions is a different process from selling to the private sector. Even if the product is right, a missing document means the bid is excluded from evaluation.

## Which documents are required in a public tender?

Purchases of traffic equipment typically require the following:

- **Declaration of compliance with the technical specification** — a written undertaking that the product meets the required characteristics
- **TSE or CE certificate** — a certificate of conformity obtained by the manufacturer
- **Sample** — some tenders require a physical sample to be submitted with the bid
- **Work experience certificate** — proof of previous sales in similar work
- **Bid bond** — usually 3% of the bid amount

Which documents are required is stated in the administrative specification. A bid should not be prepared without reading the specification.

## What should you look for in the technical specification?

Technical specifications for traffic equipment usually set out the following items: product height, base dimensions, weight, material, number and width of reflective bands, class of reflective material and colour code.

A deviation in even one of these items can lead to the bid being rejected. For example, offering a single-reflective product for a cone specified as "75 cm, double reflective, PVC, weighted base" leads to elimination.

If the specification is unclear, a written clarification can be requested before the tender date. This right is defined in Law No. 4734.

## What do reflective material classes mean?

Reflective material is classified by its coefficient of retroreflection. A higher class appears brighter under the same light and is noticed from further away.

Specifications usually contain definitions such as "Type 1", "Type 2" or "high-intensity prismatic". These definitions correspond to the classes in the EN 12899 standard.

Using a lower-class material lowers cost, but if it does not comply with the specification, the product is rejected at the inspection and acceptance stage.

## How do you obtain a work experience certificate?

A work experience certificate is requested from the purchasing institution after a sale is completed. The certificate states the subject of the work, its amount and its date.

Small sales also generate certificates. Fulfilling a small purchase for an institution provides eligibility in later, larger tenders. That is why taking on low-value jobs in the first years is strategically valuable.

The validity period and amount threshold of the certificate vary according to the type of tender.

## What is direct procurement?

Article 22 of Law No. 4734 allows purchases below a certain amount to be made directly from a supplier without a tender. This amount is updated every year.

In direct procurement, the institution carries out market price research and selects the most suitable offer. The process is fast and the documentation burden is light. First contact with public institutions is usually established this way.

Sending price lists and product catalogues to institutions' purchasing units helps you be remembered when direct procurement requests come up.
