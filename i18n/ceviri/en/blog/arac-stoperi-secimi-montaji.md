A wheel stop looks like a simple product, but choose the wrong one and either the vehicle gets damaged or the stop falls apart within six months. This guide covers the size, material and installation decisions in order.

The product goes by many names in Turkish: araç takozu, park stoperi, otopark demiri, araç durdurucu, park yeri demiri, lastik dayama. They all do the same job.

## How long should a wheel stop be?

Length is chosen according to the parking bay width and intended use.

| Length | Use |
|---|---|
| 55–60 cm | Single wheel stop, tight spaces, motorcycles |
| 110–120 cm | Standard passenger car, single stop in the centre |
| 165–190 cm | Full bay width, stops both wheels |

A standard parking bay is 2.3–2.5 metres wide. A 180 cm stop covers most of this width and ensures contact whatever angle the driver approaches from.

60 cm models are usually used in pairs, one in front of each wheel. They save material but require precise alignment.

**Height** is 10 cm on almost every model. This dimension is based on the wheel radius of passenger cars — it stops the vehicle but does not go underneath and damage the chassis.

## Rubber or metal?

**Rubber wheel stops** are made from recycled rubber. They are flexible, do not damage vehicles, are quiet and are unaffected by weather. They are the standard choice for passenger car parks.

Weight ranges from 4 to 19 kg. Being heavy is an advantage — even if a screw loosens, the stop does not shift easily.

**Metal parking stops** are usually made from Ø60 mm steel tube with electrostatic paint. They can be up to 60 cm high and act as a physical barrier. They are used to block heavy vehicle access, protect pedestrian areas and guard walls and columns.

There are also **cast aluminium** models. They are heavy, durable and attractive. They are preferred in shopping malls and corporate car parks. They cost 3-5 times as much as rubber.

| | Rubber | Metal tube | Cast aluminium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle-friendly | High | Low | Medium |
| Durability | Medium-high | Very high | High |
| Price | Low | Medium | High |
| Use | Passenger car parks | Pedestrian protection, heavy vehicles | Corporate areas |

## How is a wheel stop installed?

Installation takes five steps:

1. **Positioning.** Place the stop in the parking bay, leaving 80-90 cm from the wall or boundary. This distance accommodates the vehicle's front/rear overhang.
2. **Marking.** Mark the ground through the screw holes in the stop.
3. **Drilling.** Drill holes 90-100 mm deep with a Ø16 mm bit. Asphalt requires a hammer drill; concrete requires a rotary hammer.
4. **Inserting plugs.** Drive a Ø16×90 mm plastic wall plug into the hole. The plug should sit flush with the ground surface.
5. **Screwing.** Tighten the Ø10×110 mm lag screw with a socket wrench. Do not overtighten — crushing the rubber reduces durability.

**Four screws** are usually used per stop. 190 cm models may have six.

## Which surfaces can it be installed on?

**Asphalt** — the most common surface. Plugs hold well. Asphalt softens in summer heat, so do not overtighten.

**Concrete** — the strongest hold. Requires a rotary hammer drill.

**Paving stones** — problematic. Stones may shift and plugs may lose their grip. If possible, position the stop so that it spans more than one stone, or use adhesive support.

**Indoor car parks with floor coatings** — drilling damages epoxy or painted floors. Installation with polyurethane adhesive may be preferred, but its strength is lower than screw fixing.

## Does it need to be reflective?

It is not mandatory in enclosed, lit car parks. In open car parks, areas used at night and poorly lit locations, a model with reflective strips should be chosen.

A non-reflective black stop is invisible at night. A driver may drive over it without noticing, or a pedestrian may trip and fall. Models with yellow-black diagonal stripes are clearly visible both day and night.

## How many do you need?

The calculation is simple: **one stop per parking bay.**

A 50-car car park needs 50 units of the 180 cm stop. If the 60 cm model is used, the number doubles — 100 units.

Factor in shipping costs for bulk purchases. Wheel stops are flat, so they stack neatly and their desi is favourable. 50 units of the 180 cm rubber stop weigh about 400 kg and require transport planning.
